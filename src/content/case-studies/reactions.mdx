<Section>

Staying close increasingly requires speaking up or performing presence, and when no one does, friendships go quiet even when nothing is wrong.

Seenit had no messaging system and no social graph. I designed a reactions system that enabled response without building either — contextually relevant stickers generated from the POV text and handed off to the native share sheet.

</Section>

<Section title="Constraints">

1. No messaging infrastructure
1. Had to feel contextually relevant
1. Needed to lower the bar enough that a throwaway reply could open a real conversation

</Section>

<Section title="Lowering the cost of responding">

The beta was built around broadcasting to Instagram, however we learned almost all sharing happened in WhatsApp groups of 3–7 people, where cards consistently sparked follow-up questions and group discussion. Reactions needed to work inside those conversations.

By handing off to the native share sheet, reactions live in the same conversation where the POV is already being discussed. This let me enable reciprocity and response without building a separate messaging surface or notification layer — and test whether AI-generated reactions could meaningfully invite replies before committing to owning that infrastructure.

</Section>
