<Section>

Seenit had no messaging system and no social graph. I designed a reactions system that enabled response without building either - contextually relevant stickers generated from the POV text and handed off to the native share sheet.

</Section>

<Section>

Reactions are generated and rendered as personalised stickers, then handed off to the native share sheet. This decision was informed by beta data: 20-30% of POVs were shared, almost always inside small WhatsApp groups.

</Section>

<Section>

By leaning on existing social graphs, reactions live in the same conversations where the POV is already being discussed. This allowed me to enable reciprocity and response without building messaging or notifications prematurely, while testing whether AI-generated reactions could meaningfully invite replies before investing in owning social infrastructure.

</Section>
