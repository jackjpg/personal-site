<Section title="Spotting the blind spot">

I noticed that the final screen in the seller journey was getting 4 times more page views than the homepage. Sellers kept revisiting expecting updates that never came, they were anxious about a major asset they'd just sold. Reviews and support logs said the same thing. I asked myself: how does the lack of post-sale visibility impact user trust and satisfaction? To find out, I partnered with customer support and data teams to audit tickets and operational workflows. That's when we discovered the scale of the issue:

1. 100% of sales relied on whatsapp and phone calls, causing delays and cancellations
2. 56% of enquiries stemmed from sellers unsure of next steps

Every support call cost a few pounds. If we could reduce that traffic by giving people visibility, the savings and trust impact would be huge.

To expose where the journey was breaking down, I ran a cross-functional workshop with PMs and designers across the company. We mapped the current post-sale experience, annotated it with real seller pain points, and highlighted where ownership was fragmented or missing.

<Section title="Design challenge">

How might we improve Motorway's post-sale experience from a 'black box' into a transparent, self-serve journey that reduces operational friction. This led to 3 product goals:

1. Give sellers real-time visibility into key milestones like scheduling and payments, reducing "what's next?" uncertainty
2. Digitise manual workflows to reduce cancellations and delays caused by whatsapp and phone dependency
3. Reposition motorway as a category leader by solving an industry-wide pain point around post-sale opacity

</Section>

With the problem defined, I created a vision storyboard to show what a transparent post-sale experience could feel like. It focused on all user types in the post-sale experience, showed updates appearing in real time, payments confirmed automatically, and communication moving in-app rather than over Whatsapp.

I presented the storyboard to the executive team, framing it as both a trust strategy and a growth opportunity. The head of product championed it, and together we aligned product leads around the vision, prioritising opportunity areas across squads, balancing short-term impact with scalable bets.

This alignment led to the creation of a dedicated post-sale pillar, where I partnered with a PM and six engineers to ship the MVP.
