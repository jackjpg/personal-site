<Section>

Before users could share a single status, they needed an identity. My goal was to collapse the distance between install and identity - I collapsed a multi-step onboarding into a single tap, removing profile setup entirely.

</Section>

<Section>

I leveraged the TikTok Login Kit to fetch the user's display name and profile image, then the system passes these through a model to generate a stylised identity native to the Seenit universe. I wanted the magic of the product to be the very first thing people saw. This bypassed the usual setup hurdles, so users were looking at their first POV in seconds.

Because AI-generated identity carries a high risk of misinterpretation, I designed the UI to surface multiple generated options rather than a single correct one. To further safeguard user agency, I included a refresh trigger: allowing users to scrap the initial results and generate an entirely new set of identities. This ensured no user felt trapped by the AI interpretation, and gave a sense of ownership over how they show up in the app.

</Section>

<Section>

The same generated identity persists across all surfaces of the app, from widgets and invites to reactions, without requiring profile editing or setup.

This approach reduced backend complexity while ensuring identity was immediately recognisable and portable across the product. Users entered the product with a coherent identity already in place, without configuration or effort.

</Section>
