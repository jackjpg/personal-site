<Section>

Seenit is a mobile app that generates statuses based on your screenshots. This projects goal was to make identity feel immediately present without asking users to construct it. I focused on minimising onboarding friction while establishing an identity layer coherent with Seenit's universe – I did all design and development.

</Section>

<Section>

By using TikTok Login API, I removed profile management and collapsed a multi-step setup into a single tap. The Login Kit fetches the user's display name and profile image, then the system passes these through a model to generate a consistent, stylised identity that carries across the product surfaces, including home screen widgets, invites, and reaction stickers.

</Section>

<Section>

This approach reduced backend complexity while ensuring identity was instantly recognisable and portable across surfaces, without asking users to go through the usual account creation steps.

Because model output isn't inherently correct, the UI surfaces multiple generated options and allows users to regenerate until the identity feels right.

</Section>
